# SHIV Web Studio — Business & Creator Demo Websites Suite

A collection of **12 bespoke, mobile-first, high-performance trial websites** designed and engineered for local businesses and independent creators identified in the *SHIV Revised No Standalone Website Prospects Research (November 2026)*.

---

## 🚀 Live Architecture & Vercel Readiness

Each website is self-contained with its own semantic HTML5, custom responsive stylesheet, vanilla JS interaction layer, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, and local JSON-LD structured data.

In addition, a **Master Showcase Portal (`index.html`)** is provided at the root with:
- **Interactive Multi-Device Viewport Modal** (Test Mobile 390px, Tablet 768px, and Desktop viewports live).
- **Prospect Category Filters** (Local Businesses vs Online Creators).
- **Real-time Search Filter** by business name, keyword, or town.
- **Zero-build static serving**: Fast deployment on Vercel with zero npm build step, zero build breakage risk, and global Edge CDN distribution.

## 🎯 Design Standards & Compliance (Part C)

1. **Demo Integrity Banner**: Every single website features a clear, elegant floating disclaimer: *"Independent concept demo — not the official website"*. All simulated orders, bookings, and payments disclose that no real charge is made and route inquiry parameters straight to verified WhatsApp or social contact channels.
2. **Distinct Identities**: None of the 12 websites share a generic copy-pasted layout. Each site features distinct custom typography (`Cormorant Garamond`, `Space Grotesk`, `Teko`, `Chakra Petch`, `Playfair Display`, `Cinzel`, `Syne`, `Marcellus`, `Cabinet Grotesk`), bespoke color palettes, and unique layout architectures.
3. **Startup Animations**: Sub-900ms skippable logo/icon reveals respecting `prefers-reduced-motion` without page blocking.
4. **Complete Local & Creator SEO**:
   - High-relevance titles, meta descriptions, and keywords.
   - OpenGraph & Twitter Card tags with curated responsive images.
   - Schema.org JSON-LD Structured Data (`HairSalon`, `EntertainmentBusiness`, `ExerciseGym`, `SportingGoodsStore`, `Bakery`, `Person`, `MusicGroup`).
   - Root & individual `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`.

## 🚢 Vercel 1-Click Deployment Guide

1. **GitHub Repository**: Pushed to [https://github.com/shivamgupt-2010/shiv-deployment](https://github.com/shivamgupt-2010/shiv-deployment).
2. **Import to Vercel**:
   - Log into [Vercel](https://vercel.com).
   - Click **Add New...** &rarr; **Project**.
   - Import `shivamgupt-2010/shiv-deployment`.
   - Leave Framework Preset as **Other** (Static).
   - Click **Deploy**.
3. **Google Search Console**:
   - Open your Search Console property.
   - Submit your domain sitemap: `https://<your-vercel-domain>/sitemap.xml`.
