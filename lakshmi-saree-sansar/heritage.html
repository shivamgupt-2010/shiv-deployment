75 Years of Handloom Lineage

From the sacred ghats and pit looms of Varanasi to Shraja Mall, Burhar — three generations devoted to the preservation of Indian textile nobility.

1951

The Pit Looms of Madanpura, Varanasi

Our founder began weaving traditional Banarasi brocades using manual wooden drawlooms. Every thread of mulberry silk was wound by hand, establishing our family's reverence for pure materials and unyielding structural symmetry.

1984

Mastering the Korvai Weave of Kanchipuram

The second generation traveled south to master the intricate interlocking Korvai technique, uniting heavy mulberry silk bodies with temple-tower contrasting borders utilizing 24K gold-plated micro-zari.

2008

Establishing the Burhar Handloom Salon

Recognizing the demand for authentic, unadulterated bridal silks in the Shahdol region, Lakshmi Saree Sansar opened its doors to bring verified weaves directly to local families without middlemen inflation.

2026

The Modern Haute Couture Flagship at Shraja Mall

Today, our flagship boutique at Shraja Mall represents the pinnacle of luxury ethnic shopping: private bridal suites, virtual draping simulations, and a direct link to over 200 artisanal handloom families.

THE CONSUMER DEFENSE CHARTER

How We Guarantee 100% Authenticity

In a marketplace inundated with powerloom polyester counterfeits, here is how we protect your heirloom investment.

1. Silk Mark Organization of India

Every silk saree carries a tamper-evident holographic tag backed by the Ministry of Textiles, certifying 100% natural pure silk fibers in warp and weft.

2. The Flame Purity Test

Pure silk burns with a distinctive odor of burnt hair and leaves a brittle, crushable black bead ash. Synthetic polyesters melt into hard plastic beads. We demonstrate this test freely for any patron.

3. Real Zari Assay Certification

Our gold and silver zari threads undergo spectroscopic purity verification to ensure real copper, silver, and gold micro-plating rather than cheap aluminum dyes.
