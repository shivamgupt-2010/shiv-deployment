THE ATELIER PHILOSOPHY

Handcrafted With Reverence For India's Textile Heritage

At Lakshmi Saree Sansar, every drape narrates a century-old chronicle of artisanal mastery. Each warp and weft is meticulously spun from raw mulberry silk and interwoven with genuine metallic zari by master multigenerational karigars.

We bring the majesty of Indian royal courts to Shraja Mall, Burhar — giving every bride, matriarch, and connoisseur access to authentic heritage without traveling to metropolitan designer houses.

OUR LEGACY IN BURHAR

A Temple of Handloom Grandeur at Shraja Mall

Located at Shraja Mall, near NDC College, Burhar, Lakshmi Saree Sansar was envisioned to offer women in Shahdol district an elevated haute couture environment previously found only in metropolitan fashion districts.

We deal exclusively in authenticated handloom silks bearing verified Silk Mark accreditations. From royal Banarasi zari weaves that take 90 days on the handloom, to featherlight Chanderi and modern cutwork organza, our curators work hand-in-hand with weaving clusters across India.
