Behind The Songs

Glimpses into the stories, late-night journal pages, and inspirations.

On "Titliyan"

The Fragility of Hope

"I wrote Titliyan at 2 AM on a rainy Mumbai night when the whole city was asleep. It's about letting yourself feel without being afraid of heartbreak."

On "Kahaaniyan"

Unfinished Conversations

"Every person we meet leaves behind an unwritten chapter. Kahaaniyan is dedicated to those who loved deeply, even when distance won."

On "Subah Ki Roshni"

Healing in Silence

"Sometimes healing doesn't arrive as a grand moment—it comes like gentle early morning sunlight through bedroom blinds."
