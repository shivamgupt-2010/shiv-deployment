THE ARTISTIC JOURNEY

Soul, Sur and Devotion

Romi Mukherjee is an Indian singer, composer, and music creator whose work bridges centuries of devotional bhakti poetry with organic acoustic instrumentation. With thousands of listeners captivated across Instagram and streaming channels, Romi brings deep spiritual resonance to every live performance.

Classical Foundations: Trained in classical vocal nuances and authentic Indian ragas.
Cross-Genre Versatility: Seamlessly fluid between sacred Sanskrit stotras, Hindi devotional bhajans, and soulful indie melodies.
Spiritual Atmosphere: Known for creating meditative, elevating environments for listeners.
